Second Brain backup

A small private tool that backs up its owner's personal assistant (his notes, chats and memory) to his own Google Drive every day.

Everything is encrypted on his Mac before it is uploaded, so the files in Drive can't be read without his keys. The tool can only see the files it creates itself, nothing else in Drive.

It is used only by its owner. It is not a public service, and it doesn't collect anyone else's data.
