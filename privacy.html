Privacy policy

Second Brain backup · last updated 28 October 2026

Who uses it

Second Brain backup is a private tool used only by its owner, to back up his own data. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it can access

It asks Google only for access to the Google Drive files it creates itself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see, read or change any other file in Drive, or anything else in the Google account.

What it stores, and where

Encrypted backup archives, in the owner's own Google Drive. They are encrypted on the owner's Mac before upload; the keys never leave his devices, so neither Google nor anyone else can read the contents.

What it doesn't do

  • It collects no data from anyone else.
  • It shares, sells or transfers nothing, and uses nothing for advertising.
  • It has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party services.

Removing its access

In your Google Account, open Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Second Brain backup → delete all connections. The backup files can be deleted in Google Drive.
